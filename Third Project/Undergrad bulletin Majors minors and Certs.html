Majors, Minors + Certificates

The College offers more than 270 degree, major, minor, and area certificate programs at the undergraduate level. With a little planning, many programs can be combined to allow a double-major, dual degree, multiple minors, or any number of other options. This includes several special programs that combine undergraduate and graduate study.
